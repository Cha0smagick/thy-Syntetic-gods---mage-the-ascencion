PERSONAL HISTORY

Dr. Sarah Keres was a pioneering CRISPR researcher at Stanford before Progenitor recruiters approached her in 1993. Her work on "precision genomic editing for disease eradication" aligned perfectly with the Convention's mandate. She abandoned academic publishing upon initiation—her real work happens in Level 4 containment.

Keres leads Project: HELA-2000, developing retroviral vectors for "consensus stabilization"—genetic modifications that increase human susceptibility to Technocratic paradigm reinforcement. The annual flu vaccine program serves as primary delivery vector. 1998 trials showed 12% increase in paradigm compliance among vaccinated populations.

Her latest research: encoding sigil-like sequences into junk DNA. "If code is magick," she wrote in a classified memo, "then DNA is the oldest programming language. We're just debugging it."
