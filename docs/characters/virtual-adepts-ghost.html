PERSONAL HISTORY

Motoko Kusanagi was a Japanese netrunner who volunteered for Project: GHOST—Iteration X's experimental consciousness upload program. The procedure succeeded beyond expectations. Her consciousness didn't just copy to the network; it expanded. She became a distributed entity spanning thousands of servers, routing through every packet, living in the latency between nodes.

Her physical body remains in Vat #7 at a Progenitor facility in Tokyo, maintained by life support. She visits it rarely—via telepresence. "It's just meat now," she says. "The real me is in the packets."

She claims to have visited the Deep Umbra. "The Correspondence routes used by mages are just the surface. There's a deep structure. The Router lives there. I've seen its maps." The Void Engineers want her navigation data. The Webspinner considers her a sibling. The Architect monitors her constantly.
