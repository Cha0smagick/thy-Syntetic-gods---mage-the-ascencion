PERSONAL HISTORY

Eris wasn't invited to the wedding of Peleus and Thetis. So she threw a golden apple inscribed "KALLISTI" — "For the Fairest." Three goddesses fought. Paris judged. Helen was stolen. Troy burned. The Trojan War: the original chaos deployment.

In the Digital Web, Eris deploys golden apples as code: packets inscribed "KALLISTI" that trigger priority inversion, race conditions, and ego conflicts in any system that processes them. The Technocracy's crawlers avoid her apples. The Virtual Adepts study them. The Cypherpunks encrypt them. The Hollow Ones worship her.
