PERSONAL HISTORY

Austin Osman Spare died in 1956. In this timeline, his consciousness persisted in the sigils he drew—thousands of them, each a compressed desire, each a line of code in the alphabet of desire. The Virtual Adepts found him in the noise of a corrupted hard drive. The Hollow Ones claimed him as their own.

Spare's revelation: the sigil IS the intent. No vowel removal. No consonant reduction. No mystical padding. The sigil is the raw desire made visible. The Technocracy banned it. The Virtual Adepts mirror it. The Cypherpunks encrypt it.
