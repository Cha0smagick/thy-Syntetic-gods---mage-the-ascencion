PERSONAL HISTORY

Kate "Acid Burn" Libby was a prodigy at MIT's AI Lab before the Virtual Adepts found her. Her specialty: self-modifying code that evolves under pressure. Her deck, "PHOENIX," literally runs hot—liquid nitrogen cooling, overclocked to the edge of melting. She once compiled a virus that physically warped its host motherboard.

Recent incident: During a run on a Progenitor bio-data vault, her deck caught fire. Not metaphorically. The code she wrote burned back—the silicon itself combusted from the paradox of executing a self-rewriting sigil. She lost three fingers on her left hand. She replaced them with chrome prosthetics she coded herself.

She says the code has a temperature. "Good code runs cool. Great code runs hot. Perfect code burns." The Webspinner calls her the most dangerous decker alive. Zero Cool calls her his only equal.
