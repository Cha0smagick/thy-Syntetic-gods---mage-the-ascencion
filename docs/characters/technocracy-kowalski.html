PERSONAL HISTORY

Jan Kowalski was a Polish immigrant who joined the Chicago mob in the 1980s. The Syndicate recruited him in 1991 after he "cleaned" a scene involving a Progenitor field test gone wrong. His talent for making problems disappear—bodies, evidence, witnesses—made him invaluable.

Kowalski has 247 confirmed "cleanings" across 12 countries. His preferred method: a single .22 round, chemical dissolution, and a manufactured alibi. He refuses to use magick directly—"Magic leaves traces. Physics doesn't."

Recent assignment: eliminate the Glitch's physical manifestations. Three attempts. Three failures. The entity "glitches" out of reality before the bullet arrives. Kowalski is frustrated. He's requested X-7 "Smith" for backup. Smith denied—"Insufficient tactical value." Kowalski took it personally.
