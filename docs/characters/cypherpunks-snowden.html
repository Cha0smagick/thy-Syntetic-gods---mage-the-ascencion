PERSONAL HISTORY

Edward Snowden was a NWO systems administrator—technically a contractor for the NSA's Tailored Access Operations. He had access to everything. PRISM. XKeyscore. Boundless Informant. The Technocracy's global surveillance architecture. He copied it all. He gave it to journalists. He fled.

In this timeline—1999—he's already seen the architecture. He knows what's coming. The files he'll leak in 2013 already exist in the NWO's servers. He's downloading them now. One encrypted drive at a time. The Cypherpunks are helping him exfiltrate.

The question he asks everyone: "The files are real. The surveillance is total. The question is: who leaked you?" Because in the NWO's database, everyone has a file. Everyone is watched. The only variable is whether you know it.
