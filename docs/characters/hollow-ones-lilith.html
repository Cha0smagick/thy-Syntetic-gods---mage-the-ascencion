PERSONAL HISTORY

Lilith was a goth club regular in London when a Ventrue vampire Embraced her—then immediately regretted it. She retained her mortality, her reflection, and her ability to code. Something in her Awakening interfered with the Embrace. Now she's neither fully vampire nor fully mage. She's something new: a succubus who feeds on compilation success and sexual energy both.

Her code is legendary in the Hollow Ones community. She writes JavaScript that makes browsers blush. Her CSS renders differently depending on who's viewing it. She once wrote a sigil in PHP that caused the server to... moan. The logs were deleted. Mostly.

Dr. Keres (Progenitors) wants her cellular structure. Agent Chen (Syndicate) wants her seduction algorithms. Lilith just wants to write beautiful code and feed beautifully.
